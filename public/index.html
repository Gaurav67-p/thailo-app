Safe Investment Avenues in Nepal (नेपाल)

Low-to-moderate risk investment instruments for growing your savings securely:

1. Fixed Deposits (FD / आवधिक खाता)

Class 'A' Commercial Banks offer guaranteed returns (~7-9% p.a.). Minimal risk for preserving core savings.

2. Primary Market IPOs (MeroShare)

Apply at par value (रू 100 per share). High upside potential with small minimum capital (रू 1,000).

3. Systematic Investment Plans (SIP)

Invest monthly in open-ended mutual funds (e.g. NIBL, NMB, Siddhartha Mutual Funds) from रू 1,000/month.

4. Nepal Rastra Bank Debentures

Corporate bonds & Treasury bills backed by repute, yielding semi-annual fixed coupon payments.
